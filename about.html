Our Story

From distance to calling

A church for the curious, the hopeful, and the in-between.

Meet Math

Founder & Lead Pastor

Math didn’t grow up in church. For most of his life he was a committed atheist. Then Jesus interrupted everything.

THREE:SIXTEEN was born out of that journey. A church for sceptics, seekers and questioners. A place where you don’t have to pretend. Where faith is real, community is genuine, and everyone is welcome at the table.

If you’re curious, if you’re searching, if you’re not sure what you believe — you belong here.
