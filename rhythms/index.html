How to Pray

A.C.T.S.

A simple framework for meaningful prayer

A

Acknowledge

Start by acknowledging God for who He is — our Creator, Saviour, and King. Praise Him for what He has done. Remember His faithfulness, His love, and His presence in your life.

C

Confess

Bring the things you need to God honestly — your shortcomings, weaknesses, and struggles. He already knows, and He's ready to forgive and restore.

T

Thanksgiving

Thank God for the good gifts in your life — big and small. Gratitude rewires the heart and reminds us of His kindness every day.

S

Supplication

Ask. Bring your needs, your hopes, and the people you love before God. He invites us to cast every care on Him because He cares for us.

Start small

KEEP IT SIMPLE

1

One verse a day

Read it slowly. Write it down. Let it sit with you through the day.

2

Five minutes of prayer

Use A.C.T.S. or simply talk to God like a friend. Honest beats impressive.

3

One act of love

Faith shows up in how we treat people. Look for one chance to serve or encourage someone.
