New Mercies · Week 3

PRACTICES THAT FORM US

Following Jesus is a rhythm, not a vibe.

Sunday 17 Jan · 11:30am Acts 2:42–47

“They devoted themselves to the apostles’ teaching and to fellowship, to the breaking of bread and to prayer.”

Acts 2:42–47

Objective

Show that following Jesus is a shared rhythm, not a vibe — four practices that still make a church.

Preach points

  1. They devoted themselvesFormation takes a decision, then a habit. Devotion is not intensity for a week. It is direction for a life.
  2. Teaching, fellowship, bread, prayerFour practices. Word, one another, the table, and talking to God. This is still how C316 is built.
  3. The Lord addedGrowth is God’s work through ordinary faithfulness. We practise. He adds. We do not have to manufacture life.

Application

Pick one Acts 2 practice and do it with someone this week — open Scripture, share a meal, pray, or show up to fellowship.
