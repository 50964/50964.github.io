New Mercies · Week 1

MERCIES, NOT RESOLUTIONS

Start the year on mercy, not on shame.

Sunday 3 Jan · 11:30am Lamentations 3:22–24

“Because of the Lord’s great love we are not consumed, for his compassions never fail. They are new every morning.”

Lamentations 3:22–24

Objective

Start the year on mercy, not self-improvement theatre. Identity before activity. Grace before grit.

Preach points

  1. We are not consumedThe year begins with surviving grace. Lamentations is not a spa verse — it is mercy in the rubble. That is our starting point.
  2. New every morningYesterday’s failure does not cancel today’s mercy. Resolutions often live on shame. Mercy lives on the character of God.
  3. The Lord is my portionIdentity before activity. We do not become a people of grit first. We become a people who receive.

Application

Replace one resolution with a daily mercy: ‘Lord, I receive today.’ Write Lamentations 3:22–23 where you will see it in the morning.
