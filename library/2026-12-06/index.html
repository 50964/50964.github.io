Light for the Street · Week 1

PROMISE KEPT

Christmas is a kept promise — and a human yes.

Sunday 6 Dec · 11:30am Luke 1:26–38

“I am the Lord’s servant. May your word to me be fulfilled.”

Luke 1:26–38

Objective

Show that Christmas begins with a God who keeps his word — and with a yes from an unlikely girl in an ordinary town.

Preach points

  1. The promise lands in an ordinary placeNazareth. Mary. Not a palace. God keeps the long promise of a coming King in a room you would walk past.
  2. How will this be? — questions are welcomeMary is troubled, and she asks. Holy moments in Scripture have room for honest questions. Faith is not unthinking.
  3. A yes that makes history‘I am the Lord’s servant.’ Hope becomes flesh through obedience. Christmas is God keeping his word, and a human receiving it.

Application

Say a specific yes to God this week in one place you have been stalling. Write it down. Tell one trusted person.
