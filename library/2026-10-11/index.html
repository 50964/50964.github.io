Gone Fishing · Week 2

RESTORED

Failure is not the end of your calling.

Sunday 11 Oct · 11:30am John 21:9–17

“Simon son of John, do you love me? ... Feed my sheep.”

John 21:9–17

Objective

Preach that failure is not the end of your calling. Jesus restores the failed disciple and puts them back to work.

Preach points

  1. Breakfast for the one who denied himJesus does not begin with a lecture. He lights a fire and cooks fish. Grace arrives as hospitality before it arrives as a commission.
  2. Love is the questionThree denials, three questions. ‘Do you love me?’ is more searching than ‘How could you?’ Jesus wants the heart, not a performance of regret.
  3. Restored people are given sheepForgiveness is not a sofa. ‘Feed my sheep’ puts Peter back into the call. Mercy re-commissions.

Application

Bring your failure to Jesus this week instead of hiding it. Then do one small act of care for someone — feed a sheep.
