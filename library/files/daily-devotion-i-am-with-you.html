Daily Devotion

From YHWH Week 1 · Names & Stories

I AM
WITH YOU

Read

“So He said, ‘I will be with you. And this shall be a sign to you that I have sent you: When you have brought the people out of Egypt, you shall serve God on this mountain.’”

Exodus 3:12

“God said to Moses, ‘I am who I am. This is what you are to say to the Israelites: “I am has sent me to you.”’”

Exodus 3:14

Reflect

In the Bible study we saw that we know people by their names and their stories. Elohim is a title — God. YHWH is personal — the LORD who draws near.

At the burning bush, Moses does what many of us do when God invites us forward: he looks at himself. “Who am I?”

God does not answer with a list of Moses’ strengths. He answers with a name and a promise: I AM — and I will be with you.

The weight of the call does not rest on whether Moses feels enough. It rests on whether God is present. And He is.

Yesterday we remembered: God heard, remembered, looked, and acknowledged His people (Exodus 2:23–25). Today, take that personally. The same God who named Himself to Moses still says to the fearful and the curious: I will be with you.

Pray

LORD (YHWH), thank You that You are not a distant title but a present God. Where I am asking “Who am I?”, teach me to trust “I AM with you.” Be with me in today’s fears, decisions, and conversations. Amen.

Live it today

  • Say Exodus 3:12 out loud once this morning: “I will be with you.”
  • Name one place you feel inadequate — and deliberately invite God’s presence into it.
  • If you can, tell someone: “I’m learning God is with me, not just ‘up there’.”
