New Mercies · Week 4

WHEN COMMUNITY COSTS

Presence is a gift. Absence is easy.

Sunday 24 Jan · 11:30am Hebrews 10:24–25

“Let us consider how we may spur one another on toward love and good deeds, not giving up meeting together, as some are in the habit of doing.”

Hebrews 10:24–25

Objective

Tell the truth: real church will cost you — and presence is still a gift the world cannot replace.

Preach points

  1. Spur one another onWe do not drift into love and good deeds. We are provoked, encouraged, sometimes inconvenienced, into them.
  2. Do not give up meeting togetherAbsence is easy. Presence is a gift. The habit of staying away is named honestly in Scripture because it is old and it is deadly.
  3. All the more as you see the Day approachingThe closer the pressure, the more we need each other. Community costs because the stakes are real.

Application

Show up this Sunday. Then notice who is missing. Make one call, one message, or one knock this week.
