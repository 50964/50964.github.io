Roots · Week 4

PEOPLE OF THE WORD

The word of Christ belongs in the room, not only on the stand.

Sunday 22 Nov · 11:30am Colossians 3:12–17

“Let the message of Christ dwell among you richly as you teach and admonish one another with all wisdom.”

Colossians 3:12–17

Objective

Form C316 as a people whose shared life is soaked in the word of Christ — character put on, Scripture in the room, worship as a whole life.

Preach points

  1. Clothe yourselvesCompassion, kindness, humility, gentleness, patience, forgiveness, love. Character is put on, not waited for. The word makes a wardrobe.
  2. Let the word dwell richlyScripture belongs at the table, in the group, in the song — not only on the Sunday stand. A rich word makes a rich people.
  3. Whatever you doWord and worship spill into ordinary life. People of the Word are not experts. They are a community where Christ is at home.

Application

Put Colossians 3:12–17 on the fridge. Practise one ‘clothing’ this week — kindness, patience, or forgiveness — out loud, with someone.
