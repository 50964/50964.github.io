Love That Stays · Week 4

STILL SENT

The story does not end in the locked room.

Sunday 28 Feb · 11:30am John 20:19–23

“Peace be with you! As the Father has sent me, I am sending you.”

John 20:19–23

Objective

End the six months by sending a once-afraid church back into the world with peace and the Spirit — the story does not end in the room.

Preach points

  1. Locked doors are where he still walks inFear is not a disqualification. The risen Jesus stands among anxious people and speaks first.
  2. Peace be with youThe first Easter gift is not a task. It is peace. We are not sent as frantic people. We are sent as people who have been met.
  3. As the Father sent me, I am sending youThe story does not end in the locked room. C316 is still sent — into B62, into the street, into the next six months.

Application

Receive peace. Then go: one conversation, one invitation, one act of witness this week. The series ends. The sending does not.
