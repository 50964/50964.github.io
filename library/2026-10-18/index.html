Gone Fishing · Week 3

THE NET

The miracle is on the other side of obedience.

Sunday 18 Oct · 11:30am Luke 5:1–11

“Put out into deep water, and let down the nets for a catch.”

Luke 5:1–11

Objective

Show that empty-night effort is not the last word — the miracle sits on the other side of obedience, and encountering Jesus undoes us then sends us.

Preach points

  1. We fished all nightPeter is the expert, and the expert is tired. Effort without Jesus is exhaustion. Many of us know that night.
  2. Deep water is where he tells us to goThe command is inconvenient and specific. The catch comes as they obey, not as they discuss the odds.
  3. Go away from me, Lord — then Do not be afraidThe real miracle is not the fish. It is a holy man on his knees who is then told he will fish for people. Awe becomes calling.

Application

Do the one thing Jesus has already told you to do, even if last night was empty. Put the net down again.
