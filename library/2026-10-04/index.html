Gone Fishing · Week 1

THE CALL

Jesus still calls ordinary people from ordinary places.

Sunday 4 Oct · 11:30am Matthew 4:18–22

“Come, follow me, and I will send you out to fish for people.”

Matthew 4:18–22

Objective

Show that Jesus still calls ordinary people in ordinary places — the Christian life begins with a person, not a programme.

Preach points

  1. The call comes in the middle of workPeter and Andrew are casting nets. James and John are mending them. Jesus does not wait until we have sorted our diary. He walks into the working week.
  2. Follow me is a personThe first discipleship curriculum is Jesus himself. We do not follow a vibe, a brand, or a five-step plan. We follow him.
  3. Leaving the nets is costly and possibleThey left immediately. Something about Jesus is worth the disruption. C316 is a sent people, not a seated audience.

Application

Say yes to one next step Jesus is already putting in front of you — a conversation, a Sunday, a habit, a person to fish for.
