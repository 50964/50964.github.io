Come As You Are · Week 1

ROOM AT THE TABLE

Jesus still makes space for the ones who feel left out.

Sunday 6 Sep · 11:30am Luke 14:15–24

“Go out to the roads and country lanes and compel them to come in, so that my house will be full.”

Luke 14:15–24

Objective

Show that Jesus’ table still has room for the people who assume they are not invited — and that C316 exists to make that room visible in Halesowen.

Preach points

  1. The table is bigger than the guest listThe first invitees make excuses. Jesus is not thrown. The host keeps sending invitations. God’s welcome is not limited to the people who look ready.
  2. The people on the edge are not plan BThe poor, the crippled, the blind and the lame are the ones the servant is told to bring. The gospel is not a leftover. It is aimed at the ones who never expected a seat.
  3. There is still roomThe house is not full yet. ‘Compel them to come in’ is not force — it is urgent, personal, going-out love. C316 does not wait for people to find the door.

Application

This week, invite one person who would not assume they belong at church to sit with you on Sunday. Don’t just hope they come — go with them.
