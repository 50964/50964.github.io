New Mercies · Week 5

STAY SOFT

Stay interruptible. Stay soft.

Sunday 31 Jan · 11:30am Hebrews 3:12–15

“Today, if you hear his voice, do not harden your hearts.”

Hebrews 3:12–15

Objective

Guard against a hardened heart by practising daily encouragement and a present-tense yes to God.

Preach points

  1. Unbelief hardens slowlyIt is a drift, not a crash. The warning is for brothers and sisters, not ‘those people out there’.
  2. Encourage one another dailySoftness is a community project. We stay tender because someone speaks life into us before the heart sets.
  3. Today, if you hear his voiceThe window is now. Delayed softness becomes hardness. Stay interruptible.

Application

Ask: where am I going hard? Confess it to God and to one trusted person. Practise a ‘today’ yes.
