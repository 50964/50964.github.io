Light for the Street · Week 4

WHAT WILL YOU DO?

Meeting Jesus changes the way back.

Sunday 27 Dec · 11:30am Matthew 2:1–12

“We saw his star when it rose and have come to worship him.”

Matthew 2:1–12

Objective

After Christmas, force an honest response: worship, or notice the star and go home unchanged.

Preach points

  1. Two sets of people saw signsHerod and the Magi both hear. Only one set travels to worship. Information is not the same as response.
  2. Control or giftsHerod wants the child managed. The Magi bring gold, frankincense and myrrh. Christmas reveals what we do with Jesus.
  3. Home by another routeMeeting Jesus changes the way back. You cannot encounter the King and walk the old road as if nothing happened.

Application

Choose your gift this week: time, repentance, generosity, or a new route home. Do not leave Christmas as a spectator.
