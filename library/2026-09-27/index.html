Come As You Are · Week 4

BRING THEM WITH YOU

Faith often starts when friends carry someone to Jesus.

Sunday 27 Sep · 11:30am Mark 2:1–12

“When Jesus saw their faith, he said to the paralysed man, Son, your sins are forgiven.”

Mark 2:1–12

Objective

Show that faith is rarely a solo project — we carry people to Jesus, and Jesus sees the faith of friends.

Preach points

  1. Four friends did what he could not do aloneThe man on the mat needed carriers. Some people will meet Jesus because someone else refused to come to church empty-handed.
  2. Jesus sees their faithMark says ‘their’ faith. A church can believe for someone who is still on the way. Community is part of how healing starts.
  3. The roof comes off when love won’t waitThey could not get through the door, so they made a hole. Creative, inconvenient love is normal in the kingdom.

Application

Name one person. Ask them this week. Offer to pick them up. Faith often starts when friends carry someone to Jesus.
