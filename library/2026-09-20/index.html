Come As You Are · Week 3

THE NAME ON OUR DOOR

For God so loved the world — including Halesowen.

Sunday 20 Sep · 11:30am John 3:16–17

“For God so loved the world that he gave his one and only Son, that whoever believes in him shall not perish but have eternal life.”

John 3:16–17

Objective

Make John 3:16 the identity on the door of C316 — God loves this street, this postcode, these people, and sent Jesus to rescue, not to shame.

Preach points

  1. Loved — the gospel starts with God, not with usBefore we cleaned anything up, God loved. ‘The world’ includes Halesowen. The name on our door is not a brand first — it is a love story.
  2. Given — love that never costs is not this loveGod gave his Son. John 3:16 is not a slogan; it is a gift with a cross in the middle. We exist because of that giving.
  3. Not condemned — Jesus came to saveVerse 17 stops the church from becoming a courtroom. The curious, the doubters, the followers are met with rescue, not a raised eyebrow.

Application

Learn John 3:16 by heart this week. Say it over one person in B62 0HY by name — and invite them to the house that verse built.
