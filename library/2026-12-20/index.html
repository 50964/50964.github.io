Light for the Street · Week 3

GOOD NEWS OF GREAT JOY

Good news finds the night shift first.

Sunday 20 Dec · 11:30am Luke 2:8–20

“I bring you good news that will cause great joy for all the people. Today in the town of David a Saviour has been born to you.”

Luke 2:8–20

Objective

Preach Christmas as public good news for ordinary people — not a private religious feeling, but a Saviour announced to the night shift.

Preach points

  1. The first congregation was night-shift shepherdsGood news finds the overlooked. Heaven does not debut the announcement in a palace. It goes to the field.
  2. Great joy for all the peopleNot a few. Not the ready. All. Christmas at C316 is for the curious, the doubters, the followers, the street.
  3. They went with haste — then they toldJoy that stays silent is unfinished. The shepherds become the first preachers. We do the same.

Application

Tell one person the Christmas story in your own words this week. Invite them to C316. Joy is for passing on.
