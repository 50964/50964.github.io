New Mercies · Week 2

ONE STEP TOWARD JESUS

You do not need a five-year plan. You need one step.

Sunday 10 Jan · 11:30am Mark 1:14–20

“The time has come. The kingdom of God has come near. Repent and believe the good news!”

Mark 1:14–20

Objective

Discipleship starts with one step, not a five-year plan. God has already moved — turn, trust, and follow.

Preach points

  1. The kingdom has come nearGod has already moved. We are not climbing to him. He has stepped into the street. That is why a first step is possible.
  2. Repent and believeTurn, and trust. Not a personality transplant. A direction change toward Jesus, and a confidence in the good news.
  3. They left the nets at onceDelayed obedience is often disobedience wearing a planning meeting. One step is how a following life begins.

Application

Take one concrete step toward Jesus this week: come on Sunday, open Mark’s gospel, join Alpha, or tell someone you are following.
