Love That Stays · Week 1

LOVED FIRST

We love because he first loved us.

Sunday 7 Feb · 11:30am 1 John 4:7–12

“Dear friends, let us love one another, for love comes from God. ... We love because he first loved us.”

1 John 4:7–12

Objective

Root every act of love in the fact that we were loved first — love is received before it is performed.

Preach points

  1. Love comes from GodWe do not invent it. We do not manufacture warmth. Love is sourced in the Father.
  2. God sent his SonLove has a cost and a cross. ‘First loved’ is not a feeling; it is an event in history.
  3. If we love one another, God lives in usThe proof is in the room. A church that was loved first becomes a church that stays.

Application

Do one act of love this week that costs you — because you have already been loved. Don’t wait to feel it first.
