Come As You Are · Week 2

CURIOUS IS WELCOME

You don’t need all the answers to take a first step.

Sunday 13 Sep · 11:30am John 1:35–46

“Can anything good come from Nazareth? Come and see.”

John 1:35–46

Objective

Give permission to take a first step toward Jesus without having all the answers — curiosity is how faith often begins.

Preach points

  1. The first disciples were seekers, not expertsAndrew and the other disciple follow a question more than a lecture. Jesus turns and asks, ‘What do you want?’ He starts with desire, not a test.
  2. The invitation is Come and seePhilip does not win an argument with Nathanael. He offers an encounter. C316 is a church for the curious — you do not need certainty to walk through the door.
  3. Curiosity is not a threat to faithNathanael’s scepticism (‘Can anything good come from Nazareth?’) is welcomed, not shamed. Honest questions are a doorway, not a problem to manage.

Application

Bring your real questions this Sunday. And send one curious friend a simple text: ‘Come and see. I’ll sit with you.’
