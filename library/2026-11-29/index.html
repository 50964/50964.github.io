Roots · Week 5

ADVENT PRELUDE: HOPE

Hope does not deny the night. It names the Light.

Sunday 29 Nov · 11:30am Isaiah 9:2–7

“The people walking in darkness have seen a great light; on those living in the land of deep darkness a light has dawned.”

Isaiah 9:2–7

Objective

Begin Advent by naming the darkness honestly and announcing the Light with confidence — hope has a name and a government.

Preach points

  1. Hope does not deny the nightIsaiah starts with people walking in darkness. Advent is not fake cheer. It is a candle that tells the truth about the room.
  2. A child is born — hope has a nameWonderful Counsellor, Mighty God, Everlasting Father, Prince of Peace. Christmas is not a mood. It is a person.
  3. Of the increase of his government there will be no endHope is not seasonal. The kingdom keeps expanding. C316 lights a candle for Halesowen because the Light has already dawned.

Application

Light a candle this week for someone still walking in the dark. Pray Isaiah 9 over them by name.
