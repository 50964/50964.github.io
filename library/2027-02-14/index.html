Love That Stays · Week 2

LOVE YOUR NEIGHBOURS

Mercy crosses the road.

Sunday 14 Feb · 11:30am Luke 10:25–37

“Which of these three do you think was a neighbour to the man who fell into the hands of robbers? ... Go and do likewise.”

Luke 10:25–37

Objective

Move neighbour-love from sentiment to interruption — mercy crosses the road, and Jesus does not leave this as a story.

Preach points

  1. Who is my neighbour? often shrinks the listThe lawyer wants a boundary. Jesus tells a story that explodes the boundary. Neighbour is the one in front of you.
  2. Mercy crosses the roadLove is what we do when it is inconvenient. The Samaritan uses time, wine, oil, money, and a return visit.
  3. Go and do likewiseJesus does not leave this as a moving tale. It is a sending. B62 0HY is the road we are on.

Application

Learn the name of one neighbour this week. Cross the road for them — a knock, a meal, a lift, a listening.
