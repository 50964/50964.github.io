Roots · Week 2

WHEN GOD FEELS QUIET

You can tell God the truth and still trust him.

Sunday 8 Nov · 11:30am Psalm 13

“How long, Lord? Will you forget me forever? ... But I trust in your unfailing love.”

Psalm 13

Objective

Give language for the days God feels absent — honesty and trust can live in the same prayer.

Preach points

  1. How long is a prayer, not a failureDavid does not tidy up his feelings before he talks to God. Lament is faith that refuses to go silent.
  2. Honesty and trust can share a psalmThe same mouth that says ‘Will you forget me?’ says ‘I trust in your unfailing love.’ Mature faith is not fake peace.
  3. We keep singing because of who he isThe psalm ends in song before the circumstances have changed. We worship from the promise, not from the mood.

Application

Pray Psalm 13 out loud once this week. Don’t tidy it up. If you know someone in a ‘how long’ season, sit with them and pray it together.
