Love That Stays · Week 3

CHURCH LIKE FAMILY

Church is a people you belong to, not an event you attend.

Sunday 21 Feb · 11:30am Romans 12:9–18

“Be devoted to one another in love. Honour one another above yourselves.”

Romans 12:9–18

Objective

Cast a vision of church as a sincere, honouring, practical family — not an event you attend but a people you belong to.

Preach points

  1. Love must be sincereDrop the performance. Family that is only for Sunday smiles is not Romans 12. Hate what is evil; cling to what is good.
  2. Be devoted; honour one anotherFamily is a way of treating people. We talk up, not down. We make room. We refuse to be too busy for each other.
  3. Practise hospitality, bless, live at peaceThe small stuff is the stuff. Tables, honour, weeping and rejoicing, peace as far as it depends on you.

Application

Open your home or your Sunday row this week to someone who usually sits alone. Treat C316 like family, not like an event.
