Roots · Week 3

TRUST OVER CONTROL

Control is often fear in a smart coat.

Sunday 15 Nov · 11:30am Proverbs 3:5–6 · Romans 12:1–2

“Trust in the Lord with all your heart and lean not on your own understanding.”

Proverbs 3:5–6 · Romans 12:1–2

Objective

Trade the illusion of control for a living trust that shows up in a renewed mind and an offered life.

Preach points

  1. Trust is a whole-heart direction‘With all your heart’ is not a poster. It is a way of walking. Half-trust is just control with a prayer on top.
  2. Stop leaning on your own understandingControl is often fear in a smart coat. Proverbs is not anti-thought; it is anti-self-sufficiency.
  3. Be transformed — trust changes how we thinkRomans 12: offered bodies, renewed minds, a life that looks like worship. Trust is not a feeling. It is a new pattern.

Application

Name one thing you are gripping. Open your hands in prayer, then take the next obedient step without needing the whole map.
