Roots · Week 1

WHY THIS BOOK?

We open this book because God still speaks.

Sunday 1 Nov · 11:30am 2 Timothy 3:14–17

“All Scripture is God-breathed and is useful for teaching, rebuking, correcting and training in righteousness.”

2 Timothy 3:14–17

Objective

Build a curious church’s confidence that Scripture is God’s living word — enough to grow us, correct us, and make us ready for every good work.

Preach points

  1. Continue in what you have learnedFaith has a memory. Timothy is told to stay with the Scriptures he has known since childhood. Roots are not nostalgia; they are fuel.
  2. God-breathedThe Bible is not a relic or a self-help anthology. God speaks. That is why we open it every Sunday and why we want it in our homes.
  3. Useful — it trains us for a Jesus-shaped lifeTeaching, rebuking, correcting, training. Scripture is pastoral and practical. It does not just inform. It forms.

Application

Read this Sunday’s passage out loud every day this week. One sitting. No commentary required. Let the word do the work.
