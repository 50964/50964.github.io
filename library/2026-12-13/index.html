Light for the Street · Week 2

ROOM FOR JESUS?

The King arrives at the edge. A manger is enough.

Sunday 13 Dec · 11:30am Luke 2:1–7

“She wrapped him in cloths and placed him in a manger, because there was no guest room available for them.”

Luke 2:1–7

Objective

Ask whether there is room for Jesus in our crowded lives, homes, and church — the King arrives at the edge, and a manger is enough.

Preach points

  1. Busyness is an old problemA census fills every room. The world is crowded when the King arrives. Our diaries are not a new excuse.
  2. No guest room — the King at the edgeJesus is born where there is no space. God does not wait to be accommodated in style. He comes to the margin.
  3. A manger is enoughHe does not require perfect conditions. The question for C316 is not ‘Is our setup impressive?’ It is ‘Is there room?’

Application

Make room: clear one evening, one chair at your table, one appointment this week for Jesus — and for someone who needs a seat.
