04 // Math's story

How God
found me.

Tap each moment to read more.

  1. Happily married. Kids. A career. I didn't believe in God, and if anyone wanted to convince me about Jesus, I needed tangible proof.

  2. Then, over 12 months, God started calling me. Gently. In my heart. I told no one, not even my wife. How could Someone I didn't believe existed reach me like this?

  3. I felt His power move, just like you read in the Bible. The Day of Pentecost had fully come to me (Acts 2).

  4. From that moment I was filled with God's Spirit and started to see amazing things.

  5. I travelled to Nepal and India and saw people find Jesus, get baptised in the river and receive the power of the Holy Spirit. Me, the shy introvert. God had a plan.

  6. God asked me to get back to the book of Acts, where the gospel is told simply. Out of that, a home church was born.

  7. A small, close church meeting at Howley Grange Scout Hut. We're seasoned churchgoers and people who don't yet believe, the curious, doubters like I was, and followers of Christ. Come as you are!

Come and see.

He is very real and present. I promise.
