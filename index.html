Our story

AN ATHEISTWHO FOUNDGOD.

Math did not grow up in church. For most of his life he was a committed atheist. Faith came slowly, not in one crisis. For over a decade he has walked with Jesus. Church 316 is for people like him. The curious. The doubters. The followers. Bold on Jesus. Gentle on people.
